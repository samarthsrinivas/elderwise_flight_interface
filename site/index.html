Elderwise

A short guided check-in that estimates aging-related biomarkers, locally on your Mac.

  • Voice - pitch, jitter, shimmer, harmonics-to-noise, speech rate and pauses.
  • Vitals - heart rate, heart-rate variability and breathing rate from the webcam.
  • Eye movement - fixation stability, saccades and smooth pursuit.

Analysis runs on-device. Only speech audio (for transcription and spoken prompts) and a de-identified results summary (for a plain-English narrative) are sent to cloud services.
